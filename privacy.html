👋 The short version

  • You can play without giving us your name, email, phone number, address or photos. We never ask for them.
  • Your bowler and progress are saved on your device. If you use online features (PvP and leaderboards), your nickname, stats, rating and scores are stored online under a random anonymous ID.
  • The game shows family-safe ads from Google AdMob. You can remove them with a one-time "Remove Ads" purchase.
  • There is no chat and no way for players to send messages to each other.
  • We do not sell your data. You can ask us to delete it at any time (see Delete my data).

🧒 Children's privacy

Pin Masters RPG is made for players of all ages, including kids. We follow the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and app store family rules:

  • No personal info needed. You never need a name, email or account to play. Please use a made-up nickname, not your real name. We may change or hide nicknames that look like personal information or aren't kind.
  • Family-safe ads. Ads are requested with Google's child-directed settings and limited to general-audience (G-rated) content. Players who are kids, or whose age we don't know, get no personalized ads (ads based on their interests). We do not use ads that track kids across other apps or websites.
  • No chat or messages. Players can't talk to each other or share photos, links or contact details. Other players can only see your nickname, stats, rating and scores.
  • Purchases are behind a grown-up check. Store purchases need a parent gate in the game, plus the payment confirmation from Apple or Google.
  • Only what the game needs. The anonymous player ID and scores are used only to run the game (saving, leaderboards, PvP, security), not to advertise to kids.

Parents: if you think your child has given us personal information, email braddahzworld@outlook.com and we'll delete it. See Delete my data.

🎯 Ad choices

  • Buy Remove Ads ($0.99 one-time) to turn off banner and between-match ads. Optional "watch an ad for a reward" offers stay available but are always your choice.
  • On Android: Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads) lets you delete or reset your advertising ID.
  • On iPhone: the game doesn't ask to track you. You can also check Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EU/UK), the game will ask for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change your choice later.

⏳ How long we keep data

  • On your device: until you reset your save in the game, clear the app's data, or uninstall it.
  • Online profile and scores: while you keep playing online. Profiles with no activity for 24 months may be deleted automatically.
  • Purchase records: kept by Apple or Google under their policies, and as long as needed to restore purchases and meet tax and legal rules.

🗑️ Delete my data

1. Data on your device

In the game, tap reset save at the bottom of the home screen (or ⚙️ Settings › Erase this device's save), or uninstall the app. On the web version, clear this site's data in your browser settings. This erases your bowler, progress and settings on that device right away.

2. Online data (nickname, scores, rating, anonymous ID)

Fastest: in the game, open ⚙️ Settings › Online & Privacy › Delete my online data. This immediately deletes your online name, scores, rating, match history, friend code, invite records and anonymous ID from our server. You can also tap Erase this device's save there.

Or email us:

Email braddahzworld@outlook.com with the subject "Pin Masters RPG - Delete my data" and include:

  • your bowler's nickname, and
  • your Player ID if you can find it (shown in the game's ⚙️ Settings once online features are on).

We will delete your online profile, nickname, scores, rating and anonymous ID within 30 days and email you when it's done. Your name will be removed from leaderboards. We don't need any other personal information to do this.

3. Purchases

Payment records are kept by Apple or Google. To see or delete them, use your Apple Account or Google Account privacy settings. After your data is deleted, "Remove Ads" can still be restored with Restore Purchases because it's tied to your store account.

4. Ad data

Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (see Ad choices above). To ask Google about data AdMob holds, see Google's privacy policy.

🌎 Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example California, Texas, the EU or the UK), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your data, to object to some uses, and to withdraw consent. Email us to use any of these rights. We won't treat you differently for using them. If you're in the EU/UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

The game is run from the United States, so online data is stored and processed in the U.S. and other countries where Google operates.

🔒 Security

Online data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored in Google Firebase with access rules so players can only change their own profile. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't put personal information in your nickname.

📝 Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If a change is important (for example, collecting a new kind of data), we'll also tell you in the game before it takes effect, and we'll ask a parent's permission first if the law requires it.
